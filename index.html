80 / 20DCF / multiple weighting

Blended target ₹446 against a ₹581 price. I lean 80% on the DCF because the multiples disagree widely.

How the target is built

  • Restated the raw balance sheet, income statement and cash flow
  • Ratio and growth analysis to set the projection drivers
  • Projected free cash flow, valued two ways: perpetual growth and an average EV/EBITDA exit
  • Five multiples: EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, P/E and P/B
  • Weighted 80% on the DCF value (about ₹320) and 20% on EV/EBITDA (about ₹952)
  • The multiple ranges are wide (₹305 to ₹2,121), so the cash-flow value anchors the target

Momentum trading model: +70.7% vs the Nifty's +29.6%

Rules-based MACD and EMA200 system on 30-minute bars for Indian equities. Simulated fills include brokerage, STT and exchange fees; no real orders were placed.

Uses MACD and EMA200 signals on 30-minute bars across the NIFTY 100, Midcap and Smallcap universes.

  • Survival filter: 10 trades and a 24% win rate, set with binomial probability.
  • Costs: overnight gaps, brokerage, STT and exchange fees.
  • Capital limits: time-stops and signal ranking cut idle capital (17% of capital-days).
  • Small caps earned 60% more than large caps on 66% fewer trades.
